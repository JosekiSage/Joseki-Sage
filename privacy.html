Joseki Sage

Privacy Policy — Last updated June 2026

Overview

Joseki Sage is a browser-based Go joseki training application. It runs entirely in your browser and does not use a server, database, or any backend infrastructure. This policy describes how the optional Google Drive sync feature interacts with your data.

Data stored locally

Your joseki library, practice settings, and app preferences are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the Google Drive sync feature or export your library manually.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Joseki Sage will:

  • Request permission to create and access a single file in your Google Drive (joseki-custom-library.json) using the drive.file OAuth scope.
  • Read that file when you open the app to sync your joseki library across devices.
  • Write to that file when your library changes to keep it up to date.

The app cannot see, access, or modify any other files in your Google Drive. The drive.file scope is the narrowest permission available and restricts access strictly to files the app itself creates.

Google Drive sync is entirely optional. The app works fully without it.

Analytics

Joseki Sage uses Google Analytics (GA4) to collect anonymous usage statistics, including approximate geographic location, device type, and which features are used. This data is used solely to understand how the app is being used and to guide future development. Google Analytics data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. No personally identifiable information is collected or transmitted.

Data we do not collect

Joseki Sage does not collect, transmit, or store your joseki library data or personal information beyond the anonymous analytics described above. There are no cookies set by the app itself, and no advertising.

Third-party services

When using Google Drive sync, your data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Joseki Sage uses Google's OAuth 2.0 for authentication and the Google Drive API for file access. No other third-party services are used.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's functionality changes in ways that affect data handling. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
